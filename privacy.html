VELTRION is still being developed. This page is a comprehensive policy draft, not a legal certification or a claim that every described control is already deployed. Confirm the actual data flows and obtain qualified legal review before treating it as the final policy.

01 / SCOPE

What this policy covers

This policy describes the intended privacy principles for the VELTRION website, market-exploration interface, customer account area, support channels and related services. It applies to information provided directly by a visitor or account holder and to limited technical information generated when the service is used.

Different services or jurisdictions may require additional notices. If a feature introduces a new purpose for processing personal information, the notice should be updated before that feature is launched.

02 / INFORMATION

Information that may be handled

Information you provide

This may include your email address, profile details, support messages, account preferences, and information you submit when requesting assistance. Account registration may use an authentication provider to verify access. Never submit a password, one-time code, private key or seed phrase to a support representative.

Account and activity records

Where the relevant feature is enabled, the service may maintain account identifiers, authentication status, notification preferences, transaction requests, ledger entries and administrative audit events. These records should be limited to what is necessary to operate and secure the feature. Illustrative balances or demo activity must not be represented as verified real-world holdings.

Technical information

Servers and security tools may process basic connection details, device and browser characteristics, diagnostic logs, timestamps and information needed to prevent abuse or investigate service failures. The final policy should identify any analytics, error-reporting or advertising technologies actually used; none should be implied unless present.

Market data

The market interface may request public market data from third-party data providers. These requests can disclose technical information such as a connection's IP address to the provider. Market prices are not personal financial advice, and displayed values should include source and freshness context when available.

03 / USE

Why information may be used

Information may be processed to create and secure accounts, authenticate sessions, deliver requested functionality, show account-specific records, answer support requests, maintain service reliability, detect abuse, investigate incidents, comply with legal obligations and communicate material service changes. Optional communications should be separated from essential account and security notices.

Information should not be used for a materially different purpose without a suitable legal basis and any notice or choice required by applicable law. VELTRION should not promise a particular legal basis until the operator, jurisdiction and real processing practices have been confirmed.

04 / SHARING

Service providers and disclosures

Information may be shared with vendors that provide necessary infrastructure, authentication, email delivery, hosting, security or customer support, but only as appropriate for the service and under suitable contractual and security controls. The final policy should name the actual providers and describe relevant international transfers.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect users or the service from harm, or as part of a corporate transaction subject to applicable safeguards. Personal information should not be sold or shared for targeted advertising unless a clear, accurate notice and required legal choices are provided.

05 / SECURITY

How information should be protected

Security should include least-privilege access, protected credentials, encrypted connections, appropriate database policies, monitoring, secure backups and audit trails for sensitive administrative actions. Access to personal information should be restricted to people and systems with a legitimate operational need.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send sensitive authentication secrets over WhatsApp or email. If a data incident occurs, the operator should assess its scope, take containment steps and provide notifications when required by law.

06 / RETENTION

How long information is kept

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate operational requirements, dispute resolution and applicable legal obligations. Retention periods differ by category and should be documented by the operator. Account deletion may not immediately erase records that must be retained for legal, fraud-prevention or accounting reasons.

07 / YOUR CHOICES

Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, receive a copy of information, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or lodge a complaint with a regulator. The availability and scope of these rights depend on the circumstances.

To make a request, contact the support address below and explain the request without sending passwords or verification codes. The operator should verify identity proportionately and respond within the time required by applicable law. Do not send identity documents unless the operator has provided a verified and secure process for doing so.

08 / COOKIES

Cookies, storage and session technologies

The website may use browser storage or similar technologies to preserve authentication sessions, remember a dismissed support message, maintain essential preferences and improve reliability. Essential session technologies are different from optional analytics or advertising trackers. The deployed service should disclose each technology actually used and obtain consent where required.

09 / CHILDREN

Age and eligibility

The service is not intended for children who are not legally eligible to use it. The final operator must define age and eligibility requirements consistent with applicable laws and any financial-service restrictions. If information is believed to have been collected from an ineligible person, contact support so the issue can be assessed.

10 / CHANGES

Updates to this policy

This policy may be revised as features, providers, laws and data practices change. Material changes should be dated and communicated through appropriate channels. Continued use should not be described as consent where the law requires a separate affirmative choice.
